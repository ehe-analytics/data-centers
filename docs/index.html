Data center air quality and noise

Air quality data from Virginia DEQ's low-cost air sensors near data centers in Loudoun and Prince William counties, and from EPA's regulatory monitors for background comparison; includes a table of published noise measurements. Pulled November 5, 2026.

Daily air readings

One line per DEQ sensor location. Dark dashed line is EPA's regulatory monitor for background: PM2.5 at Broad Run HS, NO2 at Broad Run HS and James S. Long Park. EPA has no CO monitor in either county and thus has no reference.

Each location's first appearance and any gaps reflect when its sensor was in service.

Insights

  • PM2.5 levels near the data centers are generally consistent with the EPA's regulatory monitor, though with some variability.
  • Less consistency between NO2 sensor readings and the EPA monitor.
  • Not possible to compare CO sensor readings with the EPA monitor.
  • The large PM2.5 event around July 16-18 affected multiple sites, including the EPA monitor. It could be related to wildfire smoke from Canada, which news reports describe across the region on those dates (Capital Weather Gang; Potomac Local). The possibility of other contributing sources, such as a backup-generator run reported at Vantage VA2 in Sterling on July 16-17 (Virginia Mercury), cannot be eliminated without further analysis.

This is a first pass using what could be pulled quickly. Further analysis (see Potential next steps) could test these patterns.

Notes

  • Air sensors: Virginia DEQ Data Center Air Monitoring Project (Kunak AIR), hourly readings summarized to days. Days are Eastern Standard Time. A daily mean needs at least 18 valid hours; the highest-hour view shows the largest single hourly reading that day.
  • Reference: EPA Air Quality System daily summaries for the regulatory monitors. These are recent and may not be fully validated. PM2.5 uses the hourly-derived daily mean where at least 18 hours exist, otherwise the 24-hour filter value.
  • DEQ's stabilization period (before March 3) and its voided first-week PM2.5 (before March 9) are excluded for the original five locations.
  • Heritage Farm Museum is cut off after May 28: its PM2.5 went flat near zero before DEQ pulled the sensor.
  • NO2 sensor readings are low confidence: collocation with the regulatory monitor shows weak agreement. PM2.5 agreement is moderate to good.
  • CO readings here run slightly higher than DEQ's published table in places.
  • Sensors are not regulatory instruments and cannot be used to judge attainment of air standards. The two newest Prince William locations have only about ten days of data.
